一片 MEA 的
25 cm² 測試治具A 25 cm² test fixture for one AEM cell

AEM-25 是陰離子交換膜（AEM）水電解用的單電池測試治具，在 1 M KOH、60 °C 下測一片 MEA。PTL 的壓縮量由墊片決定，電流從流道板銑出的極耳導出，一片 PEEK 把 KOH 和鋁端板隔開。

AEM-25 is a single-cell test fixture for anion exchange membrane (AEM) water electrolysis. It tests one MEA in 1 M KOH at 60 °C. Gaskets set the PTL compression, current leaves through tabs machined into the flow plates, and a PEEK sheet keeps KOH away from the aluminium end plates.

    三個設計決定Three design decisions

    蛇形流道、鎳材、四線量測都是常見做法。下面這三項才決定量到的電壓能不能拿來比較。標 † 的數字沿用 Rev B 的計算值。

    A serpentine flow field, nickel parts and four-wire sensing are standard. These three choices decide whether the voltages you measure can be compared. Figures marked † carry over Rev B calculations.

    AEM-25-002

    整合式集電極耳Integrated current tabs

    2.06mΩ·cm² †

    流道板直接長出極耳，電流路徑上沒有螺栓接點。側面螺栓接點約 12–50 mΩ·cm² †，還會隨熱循環漂移，數據上看起來就像 MEA 在衰減。膜本身約 83 mΩ·cm² †。2.06 是 Rev B 用 316L、36 mm 寬的極耳算的；改成實心鎳後，即使 Rev D 的極耳縮成 28 mm，電阻仍會更低。

    The tab is part of the flow plate, so no bolted joint sits in the current path. A side-bolted contact adds about 12–50 mΩ·cm² † and drifts with thermal cycling, which looks just like MEA degradation in the data. The membrane itself is about 83 mΩ·cm² †. The 2.06 figure was calculated for the 316L plate of Rev B with a 36 mm tab; in solid nickel it stays lower even with the 28 mm tab of Rev D.

    AEM-25-003 / 004

    硬止擋墊片Hard-stop gaskets

    7.4% 標稱應變% nominal strain

    PTFE 墊片幾乎不被壓縮，流道板之間的間隙就等於墊片厚度。Rev C 拿掉流道板上的凹槽，壓縮量只剩這一個變數。組裝前量 PTL，再從 9、10、11 mil 挑組合。PTFE 長時間受壓會慢慢潛變（Rev B 評估：60–80 °C、4 MPa），所以壓縮量可能隨運轉時間略增。

    PTFE gaskets barely compress, so the flow-plate gap equals the gasket thickness. Rev C removed the pocket in the flow plate, leaving that as the only variable. Measure the PTL before each build and pick from 9, 10 and 11 mil gaskets. PTFE does creep under sustained load (the Rev B review flags 60–80 °C at 4 MPa), so compression can rise slowly over long runs.

    AEM-25-005

    全面積 PEEK 屏障Full-size PEEK barrier

    110mm 見方mm square

    絕緣片做到和端板一樣大，KOH 就碰不到鋁。它也是硬陽層之外的第二道電絕緣。10 W 時，端板到流道板的溫差算起來從約 3 K 降到 1.65 K †。

    The insulator matches the end plate, so KOH cannot reach the aluminium. It backs up the anodised layer as a second electrical insulator. At 10 W, the calculated end-plate-to-flow-plate temperature drop falls from about 3 K to 1.65 K †.
